# 11 HACKATHON RULES

1. **Time Constraint:** We have ~17 hours of effective engineering time. Optimize for speed and reliability.
2. **Blockers:** Escalate blockers immediately. Do not spend hours debugging non-critical issues.
3. **Scope:** Stick to the MVP scope. Exclude "nice-to-haves" until MVP is integrated.
4. **No Speculation:** Stop and ask the user for clarification rather than guessing requirements.

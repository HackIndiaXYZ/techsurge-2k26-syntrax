# Financial Inclusion and Responsible Framing

**External verified context from the official PS:** smallholder farmers, gig couriers, and informal workers in climate-vulnerable regions can experience severe weather-driven financial disruption. A faster predefined payment can reduce the time spent waiting for manual loss assessment.

The prototype must not imply that a dashboard solves affordability, eligibility, coverage adequacy, literacy, exclusion, or disaster response. Explain the policy in plain language, label every balance and payout `SYNTHETIC`, keep the demo accessible, and show basis risk. No real personal financial data is collected.
